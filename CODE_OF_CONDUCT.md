# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and publication of private information are not.

Keep reports factual, use fictional customer examples and respect consent and confidentiality. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation after reviewing context. Correct mistakes openly and give contributors a chance to clarify.

For private safety or confidentiality concerns, contact a repository maintainer through an available private GitHub channel. Do not publish private details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities use the route in SECURITY.md.
